# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Participants must engage constructively, welcome questions, distinguish evidence from opinion, and avoid harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss work and people with professionalism.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect private, confidential, and personally identifying information.
- Report engineering discrepancies rather than hiding them.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Concerns may be reported privately using the contact listed in `SECURITY.md`.
